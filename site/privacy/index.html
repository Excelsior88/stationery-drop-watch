Privacy · updated Nov 23, 2026

Privacy

What this site collects, who handles it, and how to reach us. In short: your email address if you sign up for alerts, plus page-view and performance statistics from Cloudflare Web Analytics. The site sets no cookies and runs no ad trackers.

Email alerts (Kit)

If you sign up for restock alerts, your email address and the trackers you pick (Hobonichi, dot grid journals, limited-edition inks) go to Kit, the service that stores the list and sends the emails. Kit sends a confirmation email first; we only add the address to the list after you click the link. We use it only to send restock alerts for the trackers you chose and occasional site updates. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to be removed. Kit's own privacy policy is at kit.com/privacy.

Kit tracks basic email opens and link clicks, for example through Kit tracking links such as click.kit-mail3.com and a standard open pixel, so we can see whether restock alerts are read and clean out inactive addresses. Those are email delivery stats, separate from this website, which sets no cookies and runs no ad trackers.

Before November 22, 2026, signups went to Buttondown, our previous email service. While we finish moving to Kit, readers who joined through Buttondown also get Hobonichi and dot grid alerts from Buttondown through December 7, 2026. From December 8, alerts go out through Kit only. After December 8, 2026, when the move from Buttondown to Kit finishes, we will delete the old Buttondown subscriber list. Buttondown's privacy policy is at buttondown.com/legal/privacy.

Clicking unsubscribe stops future alerts by placing your address on a suppression list so we don't re-email you, whereas emailing admin@stationerydropwatch.com permanently deletes your record.

We never sell, rent, or share subscriber emails. Alerts run on your consent, and you can email admin@stationerydropwatch.com anytime to request access, export, or permanent deletion. Data is processed in the United States. The site and alerts are for people 13 and older. If a child under 13 signed up, email admin@stationerydropwatch.com and we will delete it.

The signup form is protected from bots by Cloudflare Turnstile, an anti-spam check. On pages with the form, your browser loads a script from Cloudflare (challenges.cloudflare.com) and, when you submit, sends Cloudflare a token that we verify on our server. Cloudflare may look at things like your IP address and browser details to tell people from bots, and may use browser storage for that, under its privacy policy. The form goes through our own server at stationerydropwatch.com/api/subscribe, which passes your email address and chosen trackers to Kit and doesn't store them itself.

Hosting

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare handles standard request data, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

The site uses Cloudflare Web Analytics, a cookie-free analytics service from our host, Cloudflare. It loads a small script (beacon.min.js from static.cloudflareinsights.com) that counts page views and measures how pages perform, and sends those counts to this site at /cdn-cgi/rum. The statistics we can see include pages visited, referrer, browser and device type, country, and load times. Cloudflare says Web Analytics collects the minimum information needed to show how a site performs and does not track individual end users across its customers' Internet properties.

This site doesn't set its own cookies and doesn't run ad trackers.

Links to other sites

When you follow a link to a shop we track, or to Amazon, Etsy, or Teachers Pay Teachers for our own planners, journals, and printables, that site has its own privacy policy. This site doesn't set cookies to credit those visits.

We don't use affiliate links on Stationery Drop Watch.
