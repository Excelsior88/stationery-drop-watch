Who each one suits

Archer and Olive: for markers, paint and lots of size choices

Pick Archer and Olive if you want thick paper and a specific size. Its notebooks use 160gsm paper, and the A5 has 192 pages [Archer and Olive]. Sizes run from Pocket to Letter, including B5, B6, square and a traveler's notebook size [Archer and Olive]. Some designs also offer kraft (150gsm) or ivory dot grid paper [Archer and Olive]. The product page doesn't mention page numbers, an index or bookmarks, so plan to set those up yourself.

Live Archer and Olive stock

Leuchtturm1917: for writing, and the most pages

Pick the Leuchtturm1917 Hardcover Medium (A5) if you mostly write and want room. It has 251 numbered pages, a blank table of contents, two page markers, a gusseted pocket and 8 perforated sheets [Leuchtturm1917]. The paper is 80 g/m² and slightly chamois-colored, the thinnest here [Leuchtturm1917]. If you want thicker paper with the same setup, the 120G edition has 120 gsm paper and 203 numbered pages [Leuchtturm1917].

Live Leuchtturm1917 stock

Scribbles That Matter: for starting right away

Pick the Scribbles That Matter Dotted Journal Pro if you want the setup done. It comes with numbered pages, an index, a key page and a pen test page, two ribbon bookmarks, a pen loop and a 0.7 mm fineliner in the box [Scribbles That Matter]. You choose 160gsm or 120gsm paper, in A5 or B5 [Scribbles That Matter]. The Pro has a plain matte cover; the Iconic is the same idea with an embossed cover [Scribbles That Matter]. The page doesn't list a page count.

Live Scribbles That Matter stock

Notebook Therapy Tsuki: for a smaller book

Pick the Tsuki 160GSM if you want thick 160 GSM paper in a book you'll finish sooner. It's 135 × 195 mm with 128 pages, gilded edges, a 5 mm light grey dot grid, a back pocket with a sticker sheet and an elastic band [Notebook Therapy]. The product page doesn't mention page numbers, an index or bookmarks. Notebook Therapy also releases limited-edition Tsuki designs, which we track too.

Questions

What paper weight (gsm) is best for a dot grid journal?

Heavier paper is thicker. Archer and Olive, Notebook Therapy's Tsuki and the 160gsm Scribbles That Matter journals use 160gsm paper. Scribbles That Matter also sells 120gsm, and Leuchtturm1917 sells a 120 gsm edition. Leuchtturm1917's standard hardcover uses 80 g/m² paper. If you use markers or brush pens, the thicker papers are the safer pick; if you mostly write, thinner paper gives you more pages per book.

Archer and Olive vs Leuchtturm1917: what's the difference?

Paper and pages. Archer and Olive's A5 dot grid notebook has 160gsm paper and 192 pages. Leuchtturm1917's Hardcover Medium (A5) has 80 g/m² paper and 251 numbered pages, plus a blank table of contents and two page markers. Both are close to A5 in size.

Which of these dot grid journals has the most pages?

Of the four, the Leuchtturm1917 Hardcover Medium (A5) has the most pages listed: 251. Archer and Olive's A5 has 192 and the Tsuki 160GSM has 128. Scribbles That Matter doesn't list a page count on its Dotted Journal Pro page.

Which ones come with page numbers and an index?

Leuchtturm1917 (numbered pages and a blank table of contents) and Scribbles That Matter (numbered pages, an index, a key page and a pen test page). Archer and Olive's and Notebook Therapy's product pages don't mention numbered pages or an index.
