The 2026 Burgundy Edition calendar

What Diamine says is inside [1]:

  • 25 inks, every color new to Diamine's range: 24 bottles of 12 ml and one bottle of 30 ml.
  • A swatch card set and a collectable pin.
  • Eleven ink types: standard, sheen, extreme sheen, scented, shimmer, shimmer and sheen, shimmer and scent, chameleon, pigment, pigment shimmer and pigment chameleon.
  • Diamine says all of its pigment inks, shimmer and chameleon versions included, are permanent, waterproof and lightfast. Its page says this about pigment inks only.
  • Diamine's page doesn't say which day each door opens. Dromgoole's and Pen Boutique both say the 30 ml bottle is for Christmas Day [7].
  • Diamine's own UK shop listed it on Oct 26, 2026, and lists it at £82.58 ex VAT, excluded from discounts (read Nov 28, 8:26 AM ET). Diamine's listing.

Pen care for pigment and shimmer inks

The 2026 calendar includes pigment, shimmer and chameleon inks. Goldspot's listing for a Teal Edition pigment bottle (Mittens) says regular cleaning is required [4]. Cult Pens says Diamine inks, including the shimmer and sheen ones, are safe in fountain pens, and suggests rinsing the pen after use [5]. Dromgoole's says specialty inks with shimmer or other effects work best with broader nibs, and should be shaken gently before filling [6].

Questions

Where can I buy the Diamine Inkvent 2026 calendar?

At the shops on Diamine's retailer list, or at Diamine's own UK shop. At our last check (Nov 28, 8:24 AM ET), 2 of the 6 shops we check had it in stock or on preorder: The Goulet Pen Company and Cult Pens. The table above lists each shop.

What is in the Diamine Inkvent 2026 calendar?

Diamine's page says 24 bottles of 12 ml ink, one 30 ml bottle, a swatch card set and a collectable pin. All 25 colors are new to Diamine's range, in standard, sheen, extreme sheen, scented, shimmer, shimmer and sheen, shimmer and scent, chameleon, pigment, pigment shimmer and pigment chameleon types.

Are the Inkvent inks waterproof?

Diamine says all of its pigment inks, including the shimmer and chameleon versions, are permanent, waterproof and lightfast. Its Inkvent page makes that claim for pigment inks only, so check the type of each ink. Diamine does not say the other types are waterproof.

What are the Teal Edition 50 ml bottles?

Diamine sells the 25 inks from its 2025 Inkvent calendar, the Teal Edition, as 50 ml bottles. They are not the inks in the 2026 calendar.

Do pigment and shimmer inks need special care?

Yes. Goldspot's listing for a Teal Edition pigment bottle says regular cleaning is required. Cult Pens says Diamine inks, including the shimmer and sheen ones, are safe in fountain pens, and suggests rinsing the pen after use. Dromgoole's says specialty inks with shimmer or other effects work best with broader nibs and should be shaken gently before filling.
