    Good to know

    How often do you check the shops?

    About every 30 minutes. A scheduled job reads each shop's public product data, then rebuilds this site with what it found. Restock times and last-check times are written as Eastern Time. A browser that can read them shows your local time zone instead and names that zone. If it cannot, the Eastern Time label stays. Guide dates and the 2027 calendar dates stay as written.

    What do you track?

    Hobonichi Techo 2027: the Original A6 and Cousin A5 books, the English Planner A6 book, HON, Weeks and Weeks Mega, and 15 A6 covers, 12 A5 covers and 3 Weeks covers. On a separate dot grid journals page, we track a focused set of flagship dot grid notebooks from Archer and Olive, Leuchtturm1917 and Scribbles That Matter, size by size and color by color, plus Archer and Olive's dot grid refills. Notebook Therapy is listed as a link only: we don't check its stock. On a separate limited inks page, we track limited-edition fountain pen inks from Diamine, Sailor and Robert Oster, only at shops on each brand's own dealer list. Ink restocks show on that page, and you can get them by email. We don't track pens on their own, though the Sailor x Pentel advent box on the ink tracker includes writing instruments. Our Lamy release calendar lists Lamy's special and limited edition pens and the authorized shops that sell them, without stock checks.

    What do "Not yet on sale" and "Couldn't check" mean?

    "Not yet on sale" means the shop has a preview page up but isn't taking orders yet. That's different from sold out. "Couldn't check" means our check failed, for example because the shop's site didn't respond, so we don't know. We show that instead of guessing whether it's in stock.

    How do restock alerts work?

    Sign up with your email and we'll send a confirmation link. Once you confirm, you'll get free restock emails for the trackers you pick: Hobonichi, dot grid journals and limited-edition inks, plus an occasional update when we add a tracker or release something new. Ink restocks also show on the inks page. You can unsubscribe anytime.

    Which shops do you check?

    28 shops across all trackers, in separate sections per brand family. Hobonichi: 21 shops, the official Hobonichi Online Store and independent stationery shops. Dot grid journaling: 14 shops, Archer and Olive (official), Leuchtturm1917 (official) and Scribbles That Matter (official). We check Archer and Olive and Scribbles That Matter on each maker's own store, and Leuchtturm1917 at its official US store and 11 independent shops. Limited-edition inks: 8 shops, Atlas Stationers, Wonder Pens, Pen Boutique, Yoseka Stationery, The Goulet Pen Company, Dromgoole's, Cult Pens and Goldspot Pens, each on the brand's own dealer list. Some shops sell more than one brand, so they're counted once in the total. Each listing in the tracker links to the shop's own page. JetPens is listed as a link only, because its site blocks automated checks. Kinokuniya USA, Kinokuniya Singapore, Blesket Canada, Milligram and Notebook Therapy (official) are listed as links only for now: we don't check their stock, so no status is shown.

    Do you sell planners?

    Yes, our own: we make planners, journals, and printable planner inserts, and sell them on our shop page (our planners and journals). Printed books are on Amazon. Printable PDFs, including undated paper tip-ins, are on Etsy. Classroom printables are on Teachers Pay Teachers. The stationery we track is sold by the shops themselves; we just link you to them.

    Can I use alerts for reselling?

    No. Stationery Drop Watch is for people buying for their own use. Our Terms of Use prohibit using the site, its alerts or its data for reselling, scalping or bot purchasing.
