Good to know

How often do you check the shops?

About every 30 minutes. A scheduled job reads each shop's public product data, then rebuilds this site with what it found. Restock times and last-check times are written as Eastern Time. A browser that can read them shows your local time zone instead and names that zone. If it cannot, the Eastern Time label stays. Guide dates and the 2027 calendar dates stay as written.

What do you track?

Hobonichi Techo 2027: the Original A6 and Cousin A5 books, the English Planner A6 book, HON, Weeks and Weeks Mega, and a few A6 and A5 covers. On a separate dot grid journals page, we track a focused set of flagship dot grid notebooks from Archer and Olive, Leuchtturm1917, Scribbles That Matter and Notebook Therapy, size by size and color by color, plus Archer and Olive's dot grid refills. We don't track pens.

What do "Not yet on sale" and "Couldn't check" mean?

"Not yet on sale" means the shop has a preview page up but isn't taking orders yet. That's different from sold out. "Couldn't check" means our check failed, for example because the shop's site didn't respond, so we don't know. We show that instead of guessing whether it's in stock.

How do restock alerts work?

Sign up with your email and we'll send a confirmation link. Once you confirm, you'll get free restock emails from every shop we track, plus an occasional update when we add a tracker or release something new. It's one list, and you can unsubscribe anytime.

Which shops do you check?

27 shops across all trackers, in separate sections per brand family. Hobonichi: 19 shops, the official Hobonichi Online Store and independent stationery shops. Dot grid journaling: 16 shops, Archer and Olive (official), Leuchtturm1917 (official), Scribbles That Matter (official) and Notebook Therapy (official). We check Archer and Olive, Scribbles That Matter and Notebook Therapy on each maker's own store, and Leuchtturm1917 at its official US store and 12 independent shops. Some shops sell more than one brand, so they're counted once in the total. Each listing in the tracker links to the shop's own page. JetPens is listed as a link only, because its site blocks automated checks.

Do you sell planners?

Yes, our own: we make planners, journals, and printable planner inserts, and sell them on our shop page (our planners and journals). Printed books are on Amazon. Printable PDFs, including undated paper tip-ins, are on Etsy. Classroom printables are on Teachers Pay Teachers. The stationery we track is sold by the shops themselves; we just link you to them.
