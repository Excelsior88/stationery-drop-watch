Good to know

How often do you check the shops?

About every 30 minutes. A scheduled job reads each shop's public product data, then rebuilds this site with what it found.

What do you track?

Hobonichi Techo 2027 planners: the Original A6 and Cousin A5 books, Weeks and Weeks Mega, and a few A6 covers. On a separate dot grid journals page, we track a focused set of flagship dot grid notebooks from Archer and Olive, Leuchtturm1917, Scribbles That Matter and Notebook Therapy, size by size and color by color. On the Traveler's Company page, we track the five Regular size notebook colors plus limited editions: the 2027 diary accessories, anniversary sets and shop exclusives. On the MIDORI MD page, we track seasonal and limited MD releases (MIDORI's MD line is now sold as MD PRODUCT). We don't track pens or everyday refills.

What does "Couldn't check" mean?

Our check of that listing failed, for example because the shop's site didn't respond. We show that instead of guessing whether it's in stock.

What does "Not yet on sale" mean?

The shop has a preview page up for the product but isn't taking orders yet. That's different from sold out.

How do restock alerts work?

Sign up with your email and you'll get one short email when a sold-out item comes back somewhere we track. It's free and you can unsubscribe anytime.
