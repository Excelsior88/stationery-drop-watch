About Stationery Drop Watch

Limited planners sell out fast, and every shop only posts its own restocks. This site checks them all in one place.

How it works

A small script visits the public product pages of 13 stores on a schedule, at a polite pace and following each store's robots.txt. It reads whether each listing can be bought right now. When a sold-out listing becomes available again, it shows up in Restocked recently and goes out to the alert list.

We only show what the stores report. If a check fails, we say so instead of guessing.

Featured shop slot

Independent stationery shops can book the featured spot on the tracker to promote a restock or launch. It's clearly labeled as sponsored. Shops: want a featured restock slot? Email stationerydropwatch@gmail.com for rates and dates.

Affiliate links

Some links to notebook brands may become affiliate links. If you buy through one, the site may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Store listings in the tracker are plain links.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or a shop we should track? Email stationerydropwatch@gmail.com.
