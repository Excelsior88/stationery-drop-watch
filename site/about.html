About · how we check stock

About Stationery Drop Watch

Limited planners sell out fast, and every shop only posts its own restocks. This site checks them all in one place.

The list covers every restock we track, so it could be multiple emails a day. Free, and you can unsubscribe anytime. Sent via Buttondown. Privacy

The couple behind Drop Watch Coming soon

Drop Watch is run by a couple. He built the tracker after seeing how frustrating it was for his partner to keep up with planner restocks, and now they run it together. Our story and a look at the planners we use are coming soon.

How it works

A small script visits the public product pages of 27 stores about every 30 minutes, at a polite pace and following each store's robots.txt. It reads whether each listing can be bought right now. Each brand family has its own section, never mixed: the Hobonichi tracker (the official store and independent shops), dot grid journals (Archer and Olive, Leuchtturm1917, Scribbles That Matter and Notebook Therapy), Traveler's Notebook regular colors and limited editions (The Paper Mouse, Atlas Stationers, Baum-kuchen, Wonder Pens, Yoseka Stationery, Jenni Bick, Omoi Zakka Shop, Traveler's Company USA (official), Tokyo Pen Shop, The Goulet Pen Company, Simple Paper, Lovenotebooks, Blesket Canada, The Journal Shop, Buchan's Kerrisdale Stationery and M.Lovewell), MIDORI MD seasonal releases (The Paper Mouse, Bobo Design Studio, Baum-kuchen, Wonder Pens, Yoseka Stationery, Jenni Bick, Omoi Zakka Shop, Tokyo Pen Shop, MD PRODUCT USA (official), Lovenotebooks, Blesket Canada, The Journal Shop, Buchan's Kerrisdale Stationery and M.Lovewell). When a sold-out listing becomes available again, it shows up in Recent restocks and goes out to the alert list.

We only show what the stores report. If a check fails, we say so instead of guessing.

Featured shop slot

Independent stationery shops can book the featured spot on the tracker to promote a restock or launch. It's clearly labeled as sponsored. Shops: want a featured restock slot? Email admin@stationerydropwatch.com for rates and dates.

Affiliate links

Amazon links on this site are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Links to the shops we track, and to our own planners and journals on Amazon, are plain links. Buying through an Amazon link costs you nothing extra, and it never changes what the tracker shows.
