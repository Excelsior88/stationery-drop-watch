About · how we check stock

About Stationery Drop Watch

Limited planners sell out fast, and every shop only posts its own restocks. This site checks them all in one place.

One short email when a sold-out item comes back somewhere we track. No spam, unsubscribe anytime. Sent via Buttondown. Privacy

The couple behind Drop Watch Coming soon

Drop Watch is run by a couple. He built the tracker after seeing how frustrating it was for his partner to keep up with planner restocks, and now they run it together. Our story and a look at the planners we use are coming soon.

How it works

A small script visits the public product pages of 23 stores about every 30 minutes, at a polite pace and following each store's robots.txt. It reads whether each listing can be bought right now. Each brand family has its own section, never mixed: the Hobonichi tracker (the official store and independent shops), dot grid journals (Archer and Olive, Leuchtturm1917 US, Scribbles That Matter and Notebook Therapy), Traveler's Company notebooks and limited editions (The Paper Mouse, Atlas Stationers, Baum-kuchen, Jenni Bick, Traveler's Company USA (official), Tokyo Pen Shop, The Goulet Pen Company, Simple Paper and Lovenotebooks), MIDORI MD seasonal releases (The Paper Mouse, Bobo Design Studio, Baum-kuchen, Wonder Pens, Yoseka Stationery, Jenni Bick, Omoi Zakka Shop, Tokyo Pen Shop and MD PRODUCT USA (official)). When a sold-out listing becomes available again, it shows up in Recent restocks and goes out to the alert list.

We only show what the stores report. If a check fails, we say so instead of guessing.

Featured shop slot

Independent stationery shops can book the featured spot on the tracker to promote a restock or launch. It's clearly labeled as sponsored. Shops: want a featured restock slot? Email stationerydropwatch@gmail.com for rates and dates.

Affiliate links

Amazon links on this site are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Links to the shops we track, and to our own planners and journals on Amazon, are plain links. Buying through an Amazon link costs you nothing extra, and it never changes what the tracker shows.
