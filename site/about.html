About Stationery Drop Watch

Limited planners sell out fast, and every shop only posts its own restocks. This site checks them all in one place.

How it works

A small script visits the public product pages of 17 stores on a schedule, at a polite pace and following each store's robots.txt. It reads whether each listing can be bought right now. The site has two sections: Hobonichi (the official store and independent shops) and dot grid journaling (Archer and Olive, Leuchtturm1917 US, Scribbles That Matter and Notebook Therapy, each checked on its own store). When a sold-out listing becomes available again, it shows up in Restocked recently and goes out to the alert list.

We only show what the stores report. If a check fails, we say so instead of guessing.

Featured shop slot

Independent stationery shops can book the featured spot on the tracker to promote a restock or launch. It's clearly labeled as sponsored. Shops: want a featured restock slot? Email stationerydropwatch@gmail.com for rates and dates.

Affiliate links

Amazon links on this site are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Links to the shops we track, and to my own books on Amazon, are plain links that earn nothing. Buying through an Amazon link costs you nothing extra, and it never changes what the tracker shows.
