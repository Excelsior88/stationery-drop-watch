Hobonichi Techo January-start vs April-start

Hobonichi offers January-start planners and April-start editions called "Spring". Not every book comes in both versions. Which one suits you depends on when you want to begin planning and which book you want.

The short answer

  • Choose January-start if you want an English edition, the Planner, Avec, HON, Day-Free, Weeks Mega or a 5-Year Techo. None of these comes in an April-start edition.
  • Choose April-start if your year turns over in spring (school, a fiscal year, a new job) and you're happy with a Japanese-edition Original (A6), Cousin (A5) or Weeks.

Which books come in April-start

Hobonichi's help center says the Original (A6), Cousin (A5) and Weeks have April-start editions, all in Japanese.

The help center says there are no April-start editions of:

  • English editions of the Original, Cousin and Weeks
  • the Planner
  • Avec (Original or Cousin)
  • Weeks Mega
  • HON
  • Day-Free
  • the 5-Year Techo and Large 5-Year Techo
  • some special editions (it names the One Piece Original and the Moomin Weeks)

"April-start" doesn't mean it goes on sale in April

Hobonichi's help center says the April-start Spring edition "goes on sale every year on February 1st", starting at 11:00 AM Japan time on the Hobonichi Store (9:00 PM Eastern on January 31). Other shops start selling when they open.

Treat this as Hobonichi's usual sale schedule, not a separately confirmed Spring 2027 announcement. Check the official Spring announcements before planning a purchase.

Is the Spring lineup the same as January's?

Not exactly. Hobonichi says many January-start products are offered again in April-start, and "even if an item sells out in the January-start edition, it may be restocked for the April-start release." Some products are Spring-only, and the lineup can change depending on fabric availability.

So if a cover you wanted sold out in the fall, the Spring release is worth watching. Hobonichi doesn't promise it will come back.

The Sunday-start option

According to Hobonichi, the Sunday-start option is available only for the Japanese Original (A6), in both January-start and April-start editions.

How to decide

  1. When does your year start? If it's January, choose January-start. If it's April (school, work, a fiscal year), choose Spring.
  2. Do you need English? Then it's January-start only.
  3. Which format? Pick the format first. Our Original vs Cousin vs Weeks guide explains the differences.
  4. Waiting for a sold-out cover? It may come back in the Spring release, but it isn't guaranteed.

Questions

When does the April-start Hobonichi go on sale?

Hobonichi says February 1 every year at 11 AM Japan time. Check the official site for the 2027 date.

Is there an English April-start Hobonichi?

No. April-start editions are Japanese only.

Is there an April-start Weeks Mega or Day-Free?

No.
