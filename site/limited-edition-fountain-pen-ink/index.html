How we pick shops

We track a brand's limited inks only at shops that appear on that brand's own dealer list. A shop calling itself an authorized dealer isn't enough. Some shops on our other trackers sell these inks too, but if the brand doesn't list them, we leave them out.

No limited ink from these brands is on the tracker yet. We look for new ones at the shops their dealer lists name:

  • Robert Oster: Atlas Stationers, The Goulet Pen Company, Blesket Canada and Cult Pens (Robert Oster's where-to-buy page)

We also look through these shops' catalogs every day for new limited inks, including ones listed as coming soon or on preorder. A new find is checked right away, and it appears here once we've confirmed it.

Good to know

What do the statuses mean?

Statuses are exactly what each shop's public product data said at the time shown. Always confirm on the shop's page before you buy.

What do "Not yet on sale" and "Couldn't check" mean?

"Not yet on sale" means the shop has a preview page up but isn't taking orders yet. "Couldn't check" means our check failed, so we don't know.

Why don't you show prices?

We don't show prices, because they change and differ by shop. Check the shop's page.

Do restock emails cover inks?

Yes. Tick "Limited-edition inks" in the signup box and we'll email you when a tracked ink comes back. Ink restocks also show on this page.
