Drop Watch is run by Andrew Robinson. He built the tracker after seeing how frustrating it was for his partner, Arden, to keep up with planner restocks, and now they run it together. Stationery Drop Watch is built in Hamtramck, Michigan.

How it works

A small script visits the public product pages of 27 stores about every 30 minutes, at a polite pace and following each store's robots.txt. It reads whether each listing can be bought right now. On a sold-out Hobonichi Store row, a short note passes on what Hobonichi's own store data says: a restock is planned, a restock is undecided, or sales have ended. Each brand family has its own section, never mixed: the Hobonichi tracker (the official store and independent shops), dot grid journals (Archer and Olive (official), Leuchtturm1917 (official) and Scribbles That Matter (official)), and limited-edition fountain pen inks (Diamine and Sailor, only at shops on each brand's own dealer list). When a sold-out Hobonichi or dot grid listing becomes available again, it shows up in Recent restocks. Restocks from all three trackers (Hobonichi, dot grid journals and limited-edition inks) also go out by email to the people who picked that tracker. Ink restocks show on the inks page.

We only show what the stores report. If a check fails, we say so instead of guessing.
