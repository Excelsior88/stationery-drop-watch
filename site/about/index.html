About Stationery Drop Watch

Limited planners sell out fast, and every shop only posts its own restocks. This site checks them all in one place.

Stationery Drop Watch is an independent site. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any brand or shop we track. Brands or shops can ask to be removed at admin@stationerydropwatch.com.

The couple behind Drop Watch

Drop Watch is run by Andrew Robinson. He built the tracker after seeing how frustrating it was for his partner, Arden, to keep up with planner restocks, and now they run it together. Stationery Drop Watch is built in Hamtramck, Michigan.

How it works

A small script visits the public product pages of 27 stores about every 30 minutes, at a polite pace and following each store's robots.txt. It reads whether each listing can be bought right now. On a sold-out Hobonichi Store row, a short note passes on what Hobonichi's own store data says: a restock is planned, a restock is undecided, or sales have ended. Each brand family has its own section, never mixed: the Hobonichi tracker (the official store and independent shops), dot grid journals (Archer and Olive (official), Leuchtturm1917 (official), Scribbles That Matter (official) and Notebook Therapy (official)), and limited-edition fountain pen inks (Diamine and Sailor, only at shops on each brand's own dealer list). When a sold-out Hobonichi or dot grid listing becomes available again, it shows up in Recent restocks and goes out to the alert list. Ink restocks show on the inks page only; restock emails don't cover inks yet.

We only show what the stores report. If a check fails, we say so instead of guessing.

Featured shop slot

Independent stationery shops can book the featured spot on the tracker to promote a restock or launch. It's clearly labeled as sponsored. Shops: want a featured restock slot? Email admin@stationerydropwatch.com for rates and dates.
