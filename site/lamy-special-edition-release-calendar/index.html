How this calendar works

We link only to shops that are authorized Lamy retailers: Cult Pens, Dromgoole's, Goldspot Pens, Pen Boutique, The Goulet Pen Company and Yoseka Stationery. Lamy's own US store is linked on the pens it sells; it's the maker's store and we never check it. Other shops may sell these pens too.

"First listed" is the first day one of these shops put the pen up for sale. It is not an official Lamy date. When Lamy publishes a release date, we show that date instead. Upcoming pens move into Latest releases on their release date, or on the day the first shop lists them.

We don't check stock for this calendar, so there's no in-stock label here. Confirm on the shop's page before you plan around it. Each pen links to where its details come from.

Country tags show where each shop is based, not where it ships: US United States, UK United Kingdom.
