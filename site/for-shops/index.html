For shops and brands

Stationery Drop Watch is a free restock tracker for people who buy planners, journals and inks for themselves. If you run a shop, this page explains exactly what our checker does on your site, what it doesn't do, and how you stay in control.

What we do

  • We check about every 30 minutes. Each check reads the same public product information your shop already shows every visitor: whether a product or option is available, and the product title.
  • Every listing links straight to your own product page, so readers buy from you, on your site, at your price.
  • Our links carry no affiliate tag and no referral code. We don't charge shops anything, and we don't take a cut of any sale.

How our checker behaves on your site

  • It says who it is. Every request identifies itself with this User-Agent, including a link back to us and an email address:
    Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; StationeryDropWatch/0.1; Agent/StationeryDropWatch; restock tracker; +https://stationerydropwatch.com/; contact: admin@stationerydropwatch.com)
  • It follows your robots.txt. It reads your robots.txt before checking. If you add a group for StationeryDropWatch, that group is followed ahead of the general * rules.
  • It goes slowly. It makes one request at a time to your shop and waits at least 2.5 seconds between requests.
  • It asks only for what we track. In each check that's about one request per product page we track, plus a small request for your store's currency setting. Two listings on the same product page share one request. For limited inks we also read your shop's own pre-order and coming-soon lists, and once a day your public product list, to spot new limited inks.
  • It never shops. It doesn't log in, add to cart, reserve stock or check out.

Why this can't power checkout bots

A status on our site is only as fresh as our last check, which is often half an hour old, sometimes more. Checkout bots work in seconds, so a page that updates about every 30 minutes is no use to them. Our Terms also forbid using the site, its alerts or its data for reselling, bulk or automated buying, or to feed any bot or buying service.

You're in control

  • Link only. We can list your shop as a plain link with no stock checks. Once your shop is link only, our checker sends your site no requests at all.
  • Removal. We'll remove your products or your whole shop from our trackers on request (see section 12 of our Terms). Our Transparency page lists every request like this, and what we changed.
  • Block us yourself. Adding these two lines to your robots.txt stops our checks from the next check on:
    User-agent: StationeryDropWatch
    Disallow: /

Want to be tracked, or have a question?

If you'd like us to check your stock, or you'd like to talk about how we do it, email admin@stationerydropwatch.com.
