Questions

Will these still be in stock when I click?

Not guaranteed. The stock lines on this page come from our last check, and each pick links to its restock page, which shows every shop we track and updates about every 30 minutes. If something sells out, sign up for restock emails and we'll tell you when it comes back.

Which Weeks needs a cover?

None of them. Weeks books have the design on the book itself, so a cover is optional. The Original A6 and Cousin A5 books are sold without a cover.

Are the Amazon links affiliate links?

Amazon links to products we don't make are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Links to shops we track, and to our own planners and journals, are plain links.

Is the English Moomin Weeks the same as the Japanese one?

No. Hobonichi says the special specifications are for the Japanese edition only, and The Paper Mouse notes the English version doesn't include the special illustrations of the Japanese version.

Are the prices current?

They're from our last check, which runs about every 30 minutes: the lowest US-dollar price on the product data of the shops we track that had the pick in stock or on preorder. Shipping, tax and checkout discounts aren't included, and a price can change between checks, so confirm it at the shop. We don't show prices for shops that don't sell in US dollars, including Hobonichi's own store; when no price was recorded, the table says so instead of guessing. For our own books we show the Amazon list price; check the current price on Amazon.
