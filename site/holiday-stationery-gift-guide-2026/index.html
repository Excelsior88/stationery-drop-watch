Questions

Will these still be in stock when I click?

Not guaranteed. Stock and prices are from our last check, which runs about every 30 minutes; each pick's restock page shows every shop we track. If something sells out, sign up for restock emails and we'll tell you when it comes back.

Are the prices current?

They're the lowest US-dollar price at the shops we track that had the pick in stock or on preorder at our last check. Shipping, tax and checkout discounts aren't included, so confirm at the shop. Shops that don't sell in US dollars, including Hobonichi's own store, aren't priced. For our own books we show the Amazon list price.
