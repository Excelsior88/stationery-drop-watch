Privacy · updated Nov 4, 2026

Privacy

What this site collects, who handles it, and how to reach us. In short: your email address if you sign up for alerts, and cookie-free page view counts.

Email alerts (Buttondown)

If you sign up for restock alerts, your email address goes to Buttondown, the service that stores the list and sends the emails. We use it only to send the alerts and occasional updates you signed up for. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to be removed. Buttondown's own privacy policy is at buttondown.com/legal/privacy.

Page view counts (Cloudflare Web Analytics)

The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages and uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It doesn't use cookies or local storage and doesn't follow you across other sites. Cloudflare also handles standard request data, such as your IP address, to deliver and protect the site. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

This site doesn't set its own cookies and doesn't run ad trackers.

Amazon and affiliate links

Amazon links on this site are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Links to the shops we track, and to our own planners and journals on Amazon, are plain links. When you open an Amazon link, Amazon may set its own cookies to credit the referral; that happens on Amazon's side under Amazon's privacy notice. Links to the shops we track are plain links, and what you do on their sites is covered by their own policies.

Contact

Questions, or want your email address removed? Email admin@stationerydropwatch.com.
