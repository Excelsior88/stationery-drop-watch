Tips for catching a restock

  • Check more than one shop. When one store sells out, another often still has it. Right now, 4 of the products we track are sold out at one shop but still available at another. The full tracker puts every shop side by side.
  • Watch for preorders. Restocks don't always show up as "in stock". At the October 1 wave, some shops took preorders for earlier sell-outs. We count preorders as available. See the 2027 calendar.
  • Don't mistake "not yet on sale" for sold out. Shops put up preview pages before items go on sale.
  • Sign up at the official store too. Hobonichi's restock notification emails cover the Techo books and the Drawer Pouch, not accessories, and don't guarantee you'll get one [Hobonichi]. Many independent shops offer their own restock sign-ups on product pages [Paper Mouse].
  • Don't wait past December for a dated book. The Paper Mouse says it doesn't usually restock dated planners after December [Paper Mouse]. Spring (April-start) books launch in February; see the calendar.
  • Get our alerts. We check about once an hour and email you when a sold-out item comes back. Sign up above.

Questions

Where can I buy the Hobonichi Techo 2027?

At the official Hobonichi Online Store and at independent stationery shops. At our last check (Oct 29, 2026, 2:02 PM EDT), 12 of the 13 shops we track had at least one 2027 Hobonichi item in stock or on preorder. This page lists them by book and edition.

Is the Hobonichi Techo 2027 sold out?

It depends on the book, the cover design and the shop. A listing that's sold out at one shop is often still available at another, so check the tables on this page or the restock pages for each book.

Does Hobonichi send restock emails?

Yes, on its own store: you can sign up for a restock notification email when a product is sold out. Hobonichi says the service covers the Hobonichi Techo and the Drawer Pouch only, not accessories, and that the email doesn't guarantee you'll be able to buy one.

Will sold-out 2027 books come back?

Often, but not always. The Paper Mouse says almost all items have been restocked at least once after launch in past years, and that it doesn't usually restock dated planners after December.

How do I get told when something restocks?

Sign up for Stationery Drop Watch alerts. We check the shops about once an hour and send one short email when a sold-out item comes back somewhere we track.
