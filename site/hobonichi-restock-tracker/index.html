Good to know

How often do you check the shops?

About every 30 minutes. A scheduled job reads each shop's public product data, then rebuilds this site with what it found. Restock times and last-check times are written as Eastern Time. A browser that can read them shows your local time zone instead and names that zone. If it cannot, the Eastern Time label stays. Guide dates and the 2027 calendar dates stay as written.

Which shops do you check?

27 shops across all trackers, in separate sections per brand family. Hobonichi: 17 shops, the official Hobonichi Online Store and independent stationery shops. Dot grid journaling: 13 shops, Archer and Olive, Leuchtturm1917, Scribbles That Matter and Notebook Therapy, each checked on its own store. Traveler's Company: 16 shops, The Paper Mouse, Atlas Stationers, Baum-kuchen, Wonder Pens, Yoseka Stationery, Jenni Bick, Omoi Zakka Shop, Traveler's Company USA (official), Tokyo Pen Shop, The Goulet Pen Company, Simple Paper, Lovenotebooks, Blesket Canada, The Journal Shop, Buchan's Kerrisdale Stationery and M.Lovewell. MIDORI MD: 14 shops, The Paper Mouse, Bobo Design Studio, Baum-kuchen, Wonder Pens, Yoseka Stationery, Jenni Bick, Omoi Zakka Shop, Tokyo Pen Shop, MD PRODUCT USA (official), Lovenotebooks, Blesket Canada, The Journal Shop, Buchan's Kerrisdale Stationery and M.Lovewell. Some shops sell more than one brand, so they're counted once in the total. Each listing in the tracker links to the shop's own page. JetPens and Nanami Paper are listed as links only, because their sites block automated checks.

What do you track?

Hobonichi Techo 2027 planners: the Original A6 and Cousin A5 books, Weeks and Weeks Mega, and a few A6 and A5 covers. On a separate dot grid journals page, we track a focused set of flagship dot grid notebooks from Archer and Olive, Leuchtturm1917, Scribbles That Matter and Notebook Therapy, size by size and color by color, plus Archer and Olive's dot grid refills. On the Traveler's Company page, we track the five Regular size notebook colors plus limited editions: the 2027 diary accessories, anniversary sets and shop exclusives. We don't track Passport size or refills there. On the MIDORI MD page, we track seasonal and limited MD releases (MIDORI's MD line is now sold as MD PRODUCT). We don't track pens.

What does "Couldn't check" mean?

Our check of that listing failed, for example because the shop's site didn't respond. We show that instead of guessing whether it's in stock.

What does "Not yet on sale" mean?

The shop has a preview page up for the product but isn't taking orders yet. That's different from sold out.

Do you sell planners?

Yes, our own: we make planners, journals, and printable planner inserts, and sell them on our shop page (our planners and journals). Printed books are on Amazon. Printable PDFs, including undated paper tip-ins, are on Etsy. The stationery we track is sold by the shops themselves; we just link you to them. Amazon links on the site are affiliate links, and we say so on every page; links to the shops we track are plain links.

How do restock alerts work?

Sign up with your email and you'll get an email about every restock we track, so it could be multiple emails a day. It's one free list, and you can unsubscribe anytime.
