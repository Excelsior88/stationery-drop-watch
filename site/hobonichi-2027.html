Hobonichi Techo 2027 launch & restock calendar

Hobonichi sells its planners in two big drops a year, and popular covers can sell out within minutes. Here's how the 2027 season has gone so far and when stock usually comes back.

2027 timeline

  1. Aug 25, 2026

    Previews go up

    Shops post the 2027 lineup as "out of stock" or "coming soon" pages. They aren't sold out; they're just not on sale yet. [Paper Mouse]

  2. Aug 31, 2026 · 10 PM ET

    Official online launch (January-start planners)

    September 1, 2026 at 11:00 AM Japan time (August 31, 2026 at 10:00 PM ET / 9:00 PM CT) is the official Hobonichi Store's online launch time [Hobonichi] [Hobonichi, release time]. Indie shops split online and in-store times. Hobonichi's FAQ says each retail store opens at its own store hours [Hobonichi, sale schedule].
    Paper Mouse opened online Aug 31 at 10 PM ET and in-store Sep 1 at 11 AM ET [Paper Mouse].
    Atlas opened online Aug 31 at 9 PM CT, with retail expected the week of September 7. Atlas warned that popular styles and collaborations "often sell out within minutes." [Atlas]

  3. Sep 30 – Oct 1, 2026

    Second fall wave

    Hobonichi notes that some products begin sales on October 1 [Hobonichi].
    One Piece sales began October 1, 2026 at 11:00 AM Japan time (September 30, 2026 at 10:00 PM ET). One Piece 2027 editions are January-start and Monday-start only [Hobonichi].
    Paper Mouse, a US shop, listed the Weeksdays 2027 desk calendar in this wave, plus a large restock of earlier sell-outs (as preorders at some shops) [Paper Mouse].

  4. Early–mid Oct 2026

    First restocks land at indie shops

    Atlas expected its first restock in early October. Paper Mouse's shipment arrived October 15 and preorders began shipping. [Atlas] [Paper Mouse]

  5. Feb 1, 2027

    Spring launch (April-start planners)

    The official Hobonichi Store launches April-start planners on February 1, 2027 at 11:00 AM Japan time (January 31, 2027 at 9:00 PM ET) [Hobonichi]. Other shops start at their own opening hours. Hobonichi has not published the Spring 2027 cover and book lineup yet.

Which 2027 books have an April-start

April-start is only available for the Japanese Original (A6), the Japanese Cousin (A5), and the Japanese Weeks [Hobonichi] [Hobonichi].

These do not come in an April-start edition:

  • English Original, Cousin, and Weeks
  • the Planner
  • Avec
  • Weeks Mega (English and Japanese)
  • HON (Japanese and English, A6 and A5)
  • One Piece Original
  • Moomin Weeks Japanese Special Edition
  • Day-Free
  • 5-Year and Large 5-Year

Many January-start products return for April-start. Sold-out January-start items may be restocked for the April-start launch, some items are April-only, and fabric availability can change the lineup [Hobonichi]. Choosing between the two: January-start vs April-start.

Will it come back?

Usually, but not always. While some standard planners and accessories may be replenished, special or limited collaborations may only be available during the initial launch [Atlas]. Paper Mouse says almost every item has been restocked at least once after launch in past years [Paper Mouse].

Hobonichi posts restock decisions on each product page. "If there is no notice, a restock has not been decided or is under consideration" [Hobonichi]. Restock notification emails apply only to Techo books and Drawer Pouches, not accessories or stationery, and they do not guarantee a purchase [Hobonichi].

Sold-out official Hobonichi Store rows on our tracker show that product-page note directly: "Official store notes a restock is planned," "Restock undecided at official store," or "Sales ended at official store."

Ordering direct from Hobonichi (1101.com)

  • Normal dispatch is 1–3 business days, and up to 10 business days after a new release. Restocked items may take longer. Check the product page [Hobonichi].
  • Combined orders ship together once the latest item is ready [Hobonichi].
  • International orders ship from Japan via FedEx. Customs and import fees vary by country, and Hobonichi cannot estimate them in advance [Hobonichi].
  • The 2027 store-exclusive bonus is a Plain Old Acorn Charm and a 3-Color Jetstream Ballpoint Pen. It comes with an eligible Techo book bought from the official Hobonichi Store website or an official Hobonichi physical store (Tokyo Station Branch, Tobichi Tokyo/Kyoto, Hobono Eki AKAGI), while supplies last. Cover-only purchases and Avec Book 2 alone do not qualify [Hobonichi].
