# Traveler's Notebook Regular Olive (No. 15342006), research 2026-11-04

## 1. Is 15342006 genuine?
Yes. 15342006 is Traveler's Company's own product number for TRAVELER'S notebook Olive (Regular Size), and both official sites list it:
- TRC Japan: https://www.travelers-company.com/products/trnote/starter-kit-regular/olive (6,160 JPY incl. tax, "No.15342006")
- TRC USA: https://shop.travelerscompanyusa.com/products/travelers-notebook-olive ($60.00, "No.15342006", **Currently Unavailable** as of 2026-11-04)
Olive is part of the regular starter-kit color line, not a limited edition. It's listed with Black, Brown, Blue and Camel on JetPens and Tokyo Pen Shop. I did not find a release date this session.
TRC USA warns: "We have found some cases of TRAVELER'S notebook being manufactured illegally with the TRAVELER'S COMPANY logo, and currently distributed within the US... purchase directly from TRC USA or authorized resellers."
